`@halth/ui/icons` now re-exports Lucide (`lucide-react`, a new dependency) under HaltH names, one icon per job. It covers the icons the business app uses: status, actions, navigation, people and places, payments, communication, and content and settings. The existing 13 icon names are unchanged. `IconProps` is now Lucide's `LucideProps`.
